## How this works

Every lesson follows the same loop:

1. **Read a short explanation** — just enough to know what to look for.
2. **Manipulate a simulation** — move sliders, change inputs, watch the outputs.
3. **Answer questions** — the questions make you use what you just saw, not just recall a definition.

## Try it: altitude and pressure
